The whole idea in one breath

You are a business now. The IRS taxes your business's profit — money in, minus legitimate expenses — reported on Schedule C, which attaches to your normal Form 1040. Your three year-round jobs:

☐ Know what came in (royalties & sales)
☐ Know what went out, and why (receipts)
☐ Set a slice aside as you go (estimates)
☐ Confirm the gray areas with a tax pro
