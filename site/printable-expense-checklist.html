📒 Author & Small-Biz Expense Checklist

From Author Taxes, Plain English — Asdale Dynamics, LLC · Tax year ______ and month ______

Education only, not tax advice. Whether a specific expense is deductible depends on your situation and current-year rules — confirm with a qualified tax professional. Current IRS rates and thresholds live at IRS.gov.

Royalty / income sources

  • KDP statement filed (eBook / print / KU reads)
  • IngramSpark / other print distributors
  • Audiobook platforms (ACX, Findaway…)
  • Direct sales (Stripe / PayPal / marketplaces)
  • Affiliate & ad revenue
  • 1099s received — reconciled vs. my log

Marketing & ads

  • Promo sites / newsletters / launch tools
  • Social & search ads
  • Business cards, swag, launch materials

Production

  • Editing, proofing, beta copies
  • Cover art / illustration
  • Formatting & typesetting tools
  • Printing / author copies / reprint fees

COGS (physical goods & flipping)

  • Inventory bought to resell — item cost logged
  • Beginning-of-year / end-of-year inventory noted
  • Marketplace selling fees (FBA, eBay…)
  • Packaging, shipping supplies, postage

Office & gear

  • Supplies, ink, paper, keyboard, chair
  • Laptop / hardware purchases (ask pro: depreciation)
  • Software & subscriptions (business use)

Home office

  • Space measured, used regularly & exclusively
  • Rent / mortgage interest / utilities slice
  • Internet share (business-purpose %)

Travel & vehicle

  • Conferences, signings, research trips
  • Mileage log (current rate at IRS.gov)
  • Trips that mix personal & business — flagged for the pro

Professional & other

  • CPA / EA / attorney fees
  • Licensing, LLC filing, bookkeeping software
  • Courses & craft/business books
  • Bank fees (business account)
  • Payments to freelancers $600+ — ask pro re: 1099-NEC

Habits (monthly)

  • Tax slice moved to tax account
  • Every receipt captured — inbox at zero
  • Estimate paid on quarter due date
  • Estimate confirmations filed in "estimates" folder
  • Anything confusing — note it for the tax pro

Royalty log (one row per platform per month)

PlatformGrossFeesNetDeposited
 
 
 
 
 

Track gross, fees, and net separately — platform fees are deductible expenses, and deposits alone will hide them.
